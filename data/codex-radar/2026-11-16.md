## Related recent candidates

### Distillation → 2026 → October

- **2026-10-22 — [Beyond On-Policy vs. Off-Policy: At What Level Should LLM Agent Distillation Follow the Student?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31918)**
  - Authors: Ya Gao, Zhengtao Zou, Pekka Marttinen.
  - Key idea: Separates on-policiness at the interaction-history level from the token-prefix level and studies how their mixtures affect interactive agent distillation.
  - Suggested tags: `agent-distillation` `on-policy` `off-policy` `multi-turn`
  - Review status: `candidate`

- **2026-10-07 — [On-Policy Distillation Teaches New Skills but Not New Knowledge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09639)**
  - Authors: Yixuan Tang, Yi Yang.
  - Key idea: Finds that reverse-KL on-policy distillation transfers compositional reasoning skill more reliably than factual knowledge, while forward-KL changes the transfer behavior.
  - Suggested tags: `on-policy` `distillation` `reasoning` `reverse-kl`
  - Review status: `candidate`
